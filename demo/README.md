## How to Use These Demos

1. **Read the walkthrough** to understand how a complete scan unfolds from domain deconstruction to launch decision.
2. **Use as a template** for your own domain by replacing the dimensions, questions, and project goal.
3. **Reference the scoring rationale** to calibrate your own coverage thresholds and blind spot priorities.

## Quick Start: Run Your Own Scan

If you want to perform a Blind Spot Scanner on a new domain:

1. Identify your **project goal** (1 sentence)
2. Deconstruct into **5–8 core dimensions** (use `../resources/templates/` as a baseline)
3. Generate **4–6 T/F questions per dimension**
4. Score coverage (0–100%) per dimension
5. Set threshold based on risk profile
6. Produce the final report with launch decision

See the [main SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for full methodology.
