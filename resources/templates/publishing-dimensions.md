# Academic Publishing Dimensions

Use this template when the domain involves journal submission, peer review, 
or academic writing.

## Dimensions

1. **Research Ethics & Integrity**
   - Definition: Standards governing responsible conduct of research and publication
   - Key concepts: plagiarism, authorship criteria, conflict of interest, data fabrication, IRB approval
   - Safe-to-proceed: Can distinguish between acceptable reuse and plagiarism; knows COPE guidelines

2. **Journal Selection Strategy**
   - Definition: Matching research output to appropriate publication venues
   - Key concepts: impact factor, scope fit, open access models, predatory journals, acceptance rates
   - Safe-to-proceed: Can evaluate 3+ candidate journals using objective criteria beyond impact factor

3. **Manuscript Structure & Style**
   - Definition: Organizing and presenting research according to disciplinary conventions
   - Key concepts: IMRAD structure, abstract types, citation styles, supplementary materials, cover letters
   - Safe-to-proceed: Can identify structural weaknesses in a paper using a standard checklist

4. **Peer Review Navigation**
   - Definition: Responding to reviewer feedback and managing revision cycles
   - Key concepts: rebuttal letters, major vs. minor revisions, appeals process, reviewer selection
   - Safe-to-proceed: Can deconstruct reviewer comments into actionable tasks with priorities

5. **Data Management & Visualization**
   - Definition: Preparing datasets and figures that meet publication standards
   - Key concepts: FAIR principles, raw data availability, figure resolution, color blindness accessibility, reproducibility
   - Safe-to-proceed: Can produce a publication-ready figure with complete metadata and caption

6. **Copyright & Open Science**
   - Definition: Managing intellectual property and access models for published work
   - Key concepts: CC licenses, embargo periods, preprint servers, institutional repositories, APC fees
   - Safe-to-proceed: Can explain the difference between green, gold, and diamond open access
